Small-Scale Home Cat Boarding

Quiet • Safe • Cage-free home environment in Almere

Limited Availability: We offer very small-scale home-based care, usually for one household at a time (max 1–2 cats).

Home-Based Care

A True Home for Your Cat

We provide a space where your cats can relax and feel at home. Our environment is designed to be stress-free, cage-free, and full of love, just like their own home.

Active & Playful Days

Your cat won't be confined. They have the freedom to play, jump, and explore our cat-friendly living spaces. We engage with them using their favorite toys to keep their spirits high.

Quiet & Calm Nights

Rest is essential. Our home offers many cozy spots for peaceful naps. Whether they prefer a soft chair or a sunny windowsill, they'll find their perfect spot to dream.

Individual Care & Gourmet Dining

With a focus on quiet, personalized care, we welcome up to two households at a time, with a maximum of three guest cats in total. Every cat receives individual attention in a calm home environment.

  • ✨ Personalized feeding schedules
  • ✨ Daily health monitoring
  • ✨ Photo & video updates for you

Nature & Fresh Air

We enjoy safe, supervised time on our open balcony, where our guests can feel the fresh breeze and watch the birds under our constant supervision. It's a wonderful sensory experience, always shared together to ensure absolute safety.

More Than Cat Boarding

We believe cats are family.

Our service is designed for people who see their cats not simply as pets, but as beloved companions with unique personalities, emotions, routines, and needs.

We understand how difficult it can be to leave a cherished cat in someone else's care. That is why we intentionally keep our guest capacity small and focus on providing a calm, personal, home-like environment rather than a traditional high-volume boarding facility.

If you are looking for a place where your cat will be treated with patience, respect, affection, and genuine attention, you are exactly the kind of family we created this service for.

We are not the right fit for everyone.

Our service is created for cat owners who see their cats as family members and believe that emotional wellbeing, personal attention, meaningful interaction, and genuine care matter just as much as food, safety, and shelter.

Our goal is not to care for as many cats as possible.
Our goal is to care deeply for every cat we welcome.
