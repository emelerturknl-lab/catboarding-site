Privacy Notice

At Black Princess & White Prince, we value the trust you place in us when leaving your cat in our care. This notice explains how we handle your personal data in accordance with the Dutch AVG (GDPR).

1. Personal Care, Personal Data

Because we provide a home, home-based service, we only collect the information essential to the welfare of your cat and our communication with you:

  • Your name and phone number (for WhatsApp updates and coordination).
  • Your home address and emergency contact details.
  • Specific details about your cat's health, diet, and personality.

2. How We Use Information

Your details are used exclusively to manage your cat's stay, provide you with peace of mind through regular updates, and ensure we can act quickly in case of specialized care or emergencies.

3. Photos & Updates

We love sharing photos of our happy guests with their owners! We only share these with you privately via WhatsApp. We will always ask for your explicit verbal or written consent before featuring your cat on our website or social media. We never share images that reveal your identity or home location.

4. Data Storage & Sharing

As a private service, we do not sell or share your data with advertisers. Information is only shared with third parties when strictly necessary for the service (e.g., sharing health details with a veterinarian during an emergency).

5. Your Control

You have full control over your data. You can ask us to view, correct, or delete your contact information at any time. Simply send us a message on WhatsApp!
